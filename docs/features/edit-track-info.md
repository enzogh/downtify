# Edit Track Info

A song can come down with a wrong or incomplete title, artist, album or cover — a YouTube upload's title, a single filed as its own album, no year. **Edit info…** fixes a track's tags by hand, or with what Apple Music, Deezer and YouTube Music know about it.

Only admins can edit a track's info (see [Users & Sign-in](users.md#admins-and-users)). It needs the catalogs, so it isn't offered in library-only mode.

## Editing a track

1. In the **Library**, open the menu of the track (right-click, or the **⋯** button) and choose **Edit info…**.
2. The form shows the file's current tags: title, artists (separated by commas), album, album artist, year, track number, genre and the explicit mark.
3. Below it, **Suggestions** lists what Apple Music, Deezer and YouTube Music return for the song's artist and title, Apple Music first since it has the most complete tags. Search with other words to find more.
4. Click a suggestion to take its details: the fields it knows replace yours, the ones it leaves empty keep yours, and its picture becomes the new cover (**Keep the current cover** undoes that).
5. Change anything by hand, then **Save**.

The tags are written into the file the same way a download writes them. The file keeps its name and folder, so it stays in its playlists, likes and M3U files; its lyrics and any other tags are left alone. Without a new picture, the cover stays as it is.

::: tip
To fix the audio itself — another song or version under the right name — use [Replace Audio](replace-audio.md) instead.
:::
